08 / PARTNER ROLE

What a quantum-computing partner conversation would actually ask

Not the ask

“Can quantum hardware draw more analemmas or make ChiR true?”

The credible ask

“Can an alternate architecture evaluate a frozen K-01 kernel with lower time-to-solution, energy-to-solution, or total resource cost than matched classical baselines under a declared accounting boundary?”

Required evidence

Encoding and oracle construction, logical-qubit count, circuit depth, fault-tolerant operations, error correction, data movement, solution quality, failure rate, wall time, and end-to-end control, cooling, and facility energy.

09 / VERIFICATION LADDER

What this version demonstrates—and what remains unverified

1Reproducible analytic output

Deterministic Kepler solve and declared coordinate transforms. This is reproducibility of the model—not validation against nature.

2Inspectable provenance

Every export carries parameters, model tier, equations, timestamp, and all generated samples.

3Ephemeris validation

Next: compare against JPL Horizons over its valid intervals and quantify angular/time residuals.

4Deep-time authority

Requires a validated long-term orbital/precession solution. The current cycle mode is exploratory only.

5Quantum advantage

Unclaimed. It requires a defined kernel, matched classical baseline, resource accounting, and independent verification.
