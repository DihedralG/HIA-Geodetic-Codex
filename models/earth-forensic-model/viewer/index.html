Model equation

local RSL = global mean sea level + local anomaly
margin = present elevation − local RSL
P(exposed) = P(margin > 0)
What the anomaly must eventually contain

GIA deformation, gravitational fingerprints, hydro-isostasy, tectonic correction, and vertical-datum transformation. Setting it to zero produces an eustatic-only control.

How to read the observation schema

Observed records contain measurements. Derived records transform measurements through declared equations. Modeled records are simulation outputs. Hypotheses are proposed explanations. A separate support status records whether a claim is untested, provisionally supported, mixed, not supported, or falsified.
