- Brienen et al. (2012). Oxygen isotopes in tree rings are a good proxy for Amazon precipitation and El Niño-Southern Oscillation variability. *PNAS*. DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1205977109](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1205977109)

The model treats tree-ring delta-13C and delta-18O through archive-specific observation operators. Neither is a direct measurement of atmospheric oxygen concentration.

## Admission checklist

Before a source or dataset enters a production run, record:

1. full citation and persistent identifier;
2. primary data location, version, and access date;
3. license and redistribution limits;
4. spatial reference and vertical datum;
5. chronology and age-model assumptions;
6. analytical and calibration uncertainty;
7. observation operator and known confounders;
8. preprocessing code and checksum.
